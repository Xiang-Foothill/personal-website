Make AI Your Best Research Assistant

October 2025 • Literature Review, LLMs

Large language models (LLMs) have rapidly evolved into capable research assistants. This post outlines a concise workflow for using them effectively while maintaining scholarly rigor.

0) Build a Conceptual Framework First

When entering an unfamiliar field, begin by mapping its core concepts and their relationships. Converse with an LLM to extract keywords, generate analogies, and clarify how ideas fit together. As ATLAS.ti defines it, a conceptual framework “explains the main things to be studied — the key factors, variables, or constructs — and any presumed relationships among them.” This map doesn’t need to be perfect — it simply anchors your subsequent reading and analysis.

1) Keyword Search

Precise, domain-specific keywords lead to better results. Behind the scenes, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) (Lewis et al., 2020) formalizes this logic: your query is embedded into a vector space, relevant documents are retrieved and ranked, and the LLM then synthesizes from the top results. Think of keywords as coordinates — the more precise they are, the more accurately the AI navigates the research landscape.

2) Plan-Based Prompts

Provide structure before you ask for content. In LitLLMs (Agarwal et al., 2024), models that first draft an outline and then fill each section reduce hallucinated references by ≈25 %. Try:

“Create an outline summarizing recent work on [topic], grouped by methodology and year. Then expand each section using verified sources.”

This two-step prompt yields coherence and factual grounding while keeping you in control of the structure.

3) Maintain a Professional Tone

Politeness in prompts may seem irrelevant, but clear and courteous requests tend to produce more balanced outputs. As Scientific American (2024) notes, users who phrase queries politely often receive better-organized answers. Treat your LLM dialogue as professional communication with a collaborative assistant.

Conclusion

AI won’t replace scholarly judgment — it amplifies it. Start with a conceptual map, refine your keywords, and guide the model through structured, respectful prompts. Used critically, LLMs become valuable partners for faster, deeper literature research.
