Hi, I am Yuxiang Liu, a computer-science senior at the University of California, Berkeley. My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning and robotics control. More specifically, I am interested in the theoretical interpretation of physical intelligent systems and the development of generalizable high-performance robotics.

State-Conditional Adversarial Learning: An Off-Policy Visual Domain Transfer Method for End-to-End Imitation Learning

Yuxiang Liu*, Shengfan Cao*
IFAC World Congress 2026 · accepted, oral
arXiv Code
  1. Theory

    The target-domain imitation loss is upper-bounded by the source-domain loss plus a state-conditional latent KL divergence between the two observation models. This turns visual domain transfer into a tractable objective that needs no target-domain expert.

    Jt(θ) ≤ Js(θ) + α√(2γ / (1 − γ) · (𝓛(φ) + σ))

    𝓛(φ) is the state-conditional latent alignment loss that SCAL minimizes; σ is the inherent divergence between observation models.

  2. Method

    SCAL, an off-policy adversarial framework: a discriminator estimates the conditional KL term, and the encoder aligns latent distributions conditioned on system state using only off-policy, expert-free target data.

  3. Result

    Exceeds expert-supervised DAgger trajectory lengths across three target sampling distributions with as few as 256 supervision-free samples, and transfers a vision-based racing policy from low to high speed.

The Layer Mystery of VLA: An Information-Theoretical Analysis of VLA Latent Interface

Yuxiang Liu*, Lizhi Yang*, Fengze Xie*, Aaron Ames, Yisong Yue
ICLR 2027 · under review
arXiv
  1. Finding

    Fusing layers rarely helps. Across three frozen VLM backbones and two benchmarks (LIBERO, CALVIN), 47 of 54 fusion configurations underperform the best single-layer policy.

  2. Theory

    A reweighting equivalence between the information-bottleneck objective for action prediction and action-conditioned InfoNCE, which gives a principled score for how useful each layer is to the policy.

  3. Method

    InfoNCE-based layer selection uses 9–33× less GPU compute than exhaustive policy sweeps and cuts mean selection regret from 17.89 to 3.71 percentage points compared with picking the deepest layer.

VisionSE

VisionSE is an open-source library for pure-vision estimation of an ego-vehicle's motion state. By applying optical-flow measurements to consecutive image frames, the estimator recovers the motion state using only RGB input from a windshield camera.
